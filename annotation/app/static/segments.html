How to review

The chart shows the whole series. Shaded bands are the outbreak windows the segmentation method proposed. For each one, choose Accept or Reject, or drag a band's edges (or edit its dates below) to fix its start or end; a moved band counts as Adjusted. Use Add missed outbreak for an outbreak the method did not find. If the series cannot be judged (reporting artifacts, too many gaps), tick Series unusable.

A window is one outbreak, from its onset (first week clearly above baseline) to its end (last such week). Each wave is its own outbreak, including COVID-19 waves: split a window that covers two waves by adjusting it and adding the second. The faint shading either side is the 4 weeks of context stored with each outbreak; the dotted line is the method's peak. Durations outside 8–51 weeks are shown in red and will not become benchmark rows.
